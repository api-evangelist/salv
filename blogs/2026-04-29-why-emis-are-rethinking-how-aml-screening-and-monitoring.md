It's a familiar scene for Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs): a screening tool fires a hit on a customer; minutes later, the monitoring engine fires a separate alert on the same person. Two analysts work the case from different queues without realising, and before anyone connects them, an instant payment is halfway to a mule account in another jurisdiction. This is because of the AML stack most EMIs inherited from a simpler era.
