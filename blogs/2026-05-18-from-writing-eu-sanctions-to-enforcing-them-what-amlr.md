Most people who work in financial crime compliance arrive via law enforcement, audit, or legal. Siiri Graabi arrived via the European Council, where she spent over a decade as an Estonian diplomat — including time in Brussels where she was in the room as EU sanctions packages were being negotiated and written. Today, she is Sanctions and Counter-Terrorism Financing Officer at Coop Bank in Estonia.
