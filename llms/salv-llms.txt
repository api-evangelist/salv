# Salv

> Salv is a financial-crime prevention (FinCrime) SaaS platform for banks, fintechs, and payment service providers. Its AML API provides sanctions/PEP/adverse-media screening, real-time and post-event transaction monitoring, risk scoring, and collaborative intelligence sharing (Salv Bridge). Authentication is OAuth2 client-credentials (scope `aml`); production is app.salv.com and sandbox is demo.salv.com.

## APIs

- [Salv AML API](https://docs.salv.com/): 36 operations across persons, transactions, monitoring, screening, risk, alerts, custom lists, and data upload.
- [OpenAPI spec](https://docs.salv.com/api/public.yaml): OpenAPI 3.0.3, version 1.0.9.
